Step 1 of 2

01Trust SimTrack's certificate

Your phone's browser only allows the camera on secure links. SimTrack makes its own certificate for your PC — install it once and the link becomes trusted.

Download certificate

Then on iPhone / iPad:

  1. Tap Allow when Safari asks to download a profile.
  2. Open Settings → tap Profile Downloaded at the top → Install.
  3. Open Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and switch on SimTrack Local CA.

Then on Android:

  1. Open Settings → Security → Encryption & credentials (name varies slightly by phone).
  2. Tap Install a certificate → CA certificate → Install anyway.
  3. Pick simtrack-ca.crt from your Downloads.
