Privacy

Last updated 14 September 2026 · draft for review before launch

The short version

The camera image never leaves your phone. SimTrack has no account, no cloud, and no analytics. The only data that travels is three head angles per frame, from your phone to your PC, over your own WiFi.

In the app

  • Camera. Face tracking runs in your phone's browser using an on-device model. No frame is stored or transmitted.
  • Network. The phone connects to your PC on your local network using a certificate your PC creates for itself. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.
  • On your PC. SimTrack stores its certificate and a log file in your user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\SimTrack). The log contains technical status lines, never video. You may delete both at any time.
  • On your phone. Your smoothing preference is stored in the browser's local storage. That's all.

When you buy

Purchases are handled by our merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy, which collects the details needed to process payment and issue a receipt under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details to provide support and deliver updates, and we keep them only for that purpose.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Fonts are served from this site and, for the body typeface, from Google Fonts, which may log the request per Google's privacy policy.

Support

If you email us or write in the Discord, we keep the conversation to help you. We don't sell or share it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and complain to your data-protection authority. Contact details are on the main page.
